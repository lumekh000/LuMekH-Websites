YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy

We use the personal details you provide to communicate with you about your LuMekH account, orders, bookings, and requests. We do not use your personal details for unrelated purposes.

We keep the records needed to manage orders and bookings and to maintain our business records. Some order and booking records may be retained for business, tax, or accounting purposes even after account deletion.

SERVICE BOOKINGS

Booking cancellations and rescheduling

Please send cancellation or rescheduling requests at least 24 hours before your scheduled booking time. Rescheduling depends on availability. If the date you request is unavailable, you may choose another available date or request a refund.

For a cancellation made at least 24 hours in advance, the booking amount will be refunded in full. No cancellation charge or GST amount will be deducted. Refunds are sent within 7 working days.

If we cannot accommodate a requested reschedule, the booking amount will be refunded in full within 7 working days. Requests made with less than 24 hours’ notice will be reviewed individually; contact us as soon as possible.

MADE TO ORDER

Product orders and refunds

Our products are crafted for each order. Once an order is placed, it cannot be cancelled and is not eligible for a refund. Please check the product, size, colour or option, quantity, and delivery details before completing checkout.

DELIVERY

Shipping

Delivery availability and timelines depend on your location and the courier partner handling the shipment. The available delivery details will be shared for your order. If you need an update, contact us with your order number.

CUSTOMER ACCOUNT

Account deletion

Email accountdelete@lumekh.com from the email address linked to your account. Include your full name and request account deletion. We will confirm that we received your request.

You may withdraw the request within 30 days of our confirmation by emailing us again. If you do not withdraw it, we will delete the account within the following 10 days. Account and profile details eligible for deletion will be removed; order and booking records may be retained for business, tax, or accounting purposes.
